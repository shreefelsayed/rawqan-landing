Delete your Rwaqan account

You can permanently delete your Rwaqan account from inside the app or by contacting support. This page explains the steps and what happens to your data — including the information Google Play requires for account deletion.

Apps: Rwaqan Customer (com.rwaqan.customer) & Rwaqan Seller (com.rwaqan.seller) Last updated: 21 November 2026

On this page

Important

Account deletion is permanent. You will lose access to your profile, saved addresses, wishlist, order history in the app, and seller store data linked to that account. Outstanding orders should be completed or cancelled before you delete.

1. Delete your account in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Rwaqan Customer or Seller app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile / Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted. Deletion cannot be undone.

After confirmation, we run our account-deletion process and sign you out. You will see a confirmation when it completes successfully.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you cannot use the in-app flow, email us from the address linked to your Rwaqan account:

Email deletion request

To: khazeen.elbait.alex@gmail.com
Subject: Delete Rwaqan account
Include: the phone number or email on your account, whether you use the Customer or Seller app, and a clear request to delete the account.

We will verify ownership of the account and process the request. You can also reach us by WhatsApp or phone at +20 102 428 5147.

3. Data deleted when you delete your account

When deletion completes, we delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account, including:

  • Account profile (name, email, phone, profile photo)
  • Authentication / sign-in credentials for that account
  • Saved delivery addresses and map locations
  • Wishlist and cart data
  • Push notification tokens
  • In-app preferences (such as language)
  • For sellers: store profile data and product listings tied to that seller account (where not required for other users’ completed orders)

4. Data that may be retained

Some information may be kept for a limited period when required for legal, security, accounting, or dispute-resolution reasons:

  • Completed order / transaction records — needed for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, and resolving delivery or payment disputes. Personal identifiers may be reduced or replaced where possible.
  • Financial / payout records for sellers — retained as required for bookkeeping and compliance.
  • Support correspondence related to the deletion request or prior disputes.
  • Security logs that do not identify you beyond what is needed to detect abuse.

Retained records are kept only as long as needed for those purposes (typically up to 24 months, or longer if Egyptian law requires), then deleted or fully anonymized.

5. How long deletion takes

  • In-app deletion: usually completes immediately after you confirm.
  • Email / support request: we aim to process verified requests within 7 days.
  • Backup systems may take additional time (up to 30 days) to clear residual copies.
