Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Rwaqan (رواقان) collects, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective: 21 November 2026 Applies to: Rwaqan Customer & Seller apps and rwaqan.com

On this page

Who we are Information we collect How we use information Sharing Retention Security Your rights Children Changes Contact

1. Who we are

Rwaqan is an Egyptian marketplace platform that lets customers browse and buy from local stores and brands, and lets sellers manage products, orders, and payouts. When we say “Rwaqan,” “we,” or “us,” we mean the operator of the Rwaqan Customer app, Rwaqan Seller app, and this website.

2. Information we collect

Account & profile

  • Name, email address, phone number, and profile photo (if you provide one).
  • Sign-in details when you use email/password, Google, or Apple Sign-In.

Orders & delivery

  • Delivery addresses, saved locations, and map pins you set for delivery.
  • Order history, cart contents, wishlist items, discount codes used, and delivery preferences (now or scheduled).
  • Payment method choice (card or cash on delivery). Card payments are processed by our payment partner Kashier; we do not store full card numbers on our servers.

Seller information

  • Store or brand details, product listings, order status updates, wallet deposits/withdrawals, and commission-related records.

Device & usage

  • App notifications tokens (to send push notifications about orders).
  • Basic technical data such as device type, app version, language preference (Arabic/English), and crash or diagnostic logs needed to keep the service reliable.

Support

  • Messages, call, WhatsApp, or email content you send when contacting support.

3. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate sign-in.
  • Process orders, deliveries, payments, refunds, and seller payouts.
  • Show relevant stores, products, search results, and promotions.
  • Send transactional notifications (order updates, delivery status).
  • Provide customer and seller support.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security incidents; enforce our Terms.
  • Improve the apps, fix bugs, and understand aggregate usage.
  • Comply with Egyptian law and respond to lawful requests.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to operate Rwaqan:

  • Sellers / stores — receive the order and delivery details needed to fulfill your purchase.
  • Delivery partners / couriers — receive address and contact details needed for delivery.
  • Payment processors — Kashier processes card payments on our behalf.
  • Infrastructure providers — cloud and authentication services that host and secure the apps (for example Firebase-related services).
  • Legal / safety — when required by law, or to protect users, sellers, or Rwaqan’s rights.

5. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including order history for customer support, accounting, dispute handling, and legal compliance. When you delete your account, we remove or anonymize personal data associated with the account as described on our Delete Account page, except where we must retain limited records (for example completed transactions) for legal or financial obligations.

6. Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and authenticated cloud backends. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please protect your device and login credentials.

7. Your rights & choices

  • Update profile details inside the app settings.
  • Turn off push notifications in your device settings.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us or using in-app account deletion (see Delete Account).
  • Stop using the service and uninstall the apps at any time.

8. Children

Rwaqan is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective” date above will change when we do. Continued use of Rwaqan after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
