FAQ

Good to know

What is Rwaqan?

Rwaqan is an Egyptian marketplace app that lets you browse and buy products from local stores and brands in one place, then have them delivered to your door.

How can I pay?

You can pay with Cash on Delivery, or online by card — Visa and Mastercard — with payments processed securely through Kashier.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Rwaqan delivers across supported cities in Egypt. The available delivery areas are shown at checkout based on your saved address.

Is the app in Arabic?

Yes — Rwaqan is fully bilingual and switches between Arabic and English in a single tap.

Can I schedule my delivery?

Absolutely. Choose “Deliver now” for the soonest slot, or pick a custom delivery time that fits your schedule.

How do I sell on Rwaqan?

Download the Rwaqan Seller app to list your products, receive and manage orders, and withdraw earnings from your in-app wallet.
